Get alerts firing or observed recently. Prefer time_iso (RFC3339) over the deprecated timestamp alias. window is seconds (default 900, range 1-3600); lookback_minutes is used only when window is absent (range 1-60). Cap longer requests at one hour.

Returns timestamp, window, and alert_rules with alert-group/rule identity, severity, state, rule_type, since (first observed firing), last_fired_at (last observed firing), and instances with labels, annotations, degradation, and current_value. last_fired_at is not confirmed resolution. For adaptive expressions, current_value may be comparator output (for example 1), not the observed metric.
