Dry-run an alert rule expression against an alert group's KPIs without saving anything. Use it before create_alert or update_alert. Parameters: entity_id (required, alert group/entity UUID), expression (required, arithmetic over indicator names such as x, y, z; at most 3 indicators), indicators_used (map of each indicator name used in the expression to its KPI UUID owned by entity_id; get the KPI UUIDs from get_alert_config or get_entity_alert_rules). The expression is also evaluated against the alert group's data source and must yield at least one timeseries. Returns {"valid":true} or {"valid":false,"reason":"..."} describing why it is invalid; an invalid expression is a normal result, not a tool error.
