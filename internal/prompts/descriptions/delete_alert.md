Permanently delete a Last9 alert rule from its owning alert group. Use get_alert_config first to verify entity_id, the exact rule ID, notification coverage, runbook/links, and whether another rule depends on its KPI. Summarize the operational impact and only call this tool after the user explicitly confirms deletion. It first checks that the rule exists and belongs to entity_id, and refuses without deleting anything otherwise. Deletion requires a delete-scope token (a write-scope token gets 403) and does not promise cleanup of a separately managed KPI. Returns deletion status and a link to Alerting.
