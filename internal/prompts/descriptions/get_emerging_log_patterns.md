Answer "what is new or different in this service's logs right now?" Clusters a current and a baseline window into message templates (variables become `<*>`) and returns only new, increased, decreased or disappeared ones, with counts from both. Use it first in triage instead of paging raw lines with `get_service_logs` or `get_logs`; read raw lines only to drill into a template. For one window, use `get_log_patterns`.

Returns:
- `current` and `baseline`: each window's range, `sampled_lines`, `sample_limit_reached` (the sample may miss lines), `total_matching_lines` when reported, and `logs_truncated` when the search stopped early.
- `comparison_basis`: `rate_per_minute` when both samples are complete; `estimated_rate_per_minute` when a capped sample is scaled to its exact `total_matching_lines`; `share_of_sample` when a capped window's volume is unknown (no total, or `logs_truncated`).
- `patterns`: each with `template`, `change` (`new`, `increased`, `decreased`, `disappeared`), `ratio` (current over baseline frequency, when both are non-zero), `current` and `baseline` stats (`count`, `first_seen`, `last_seen`, `severity_counts`) and one raw `sample` line, ordered new, increased, decreased, disappeared.
- `unchanged_patterns`: templates in both windows that changed by less than `change_factor`. `below_min_count_patterns`: templates that changed (including new or disappeared ones) but had fewer than `min_count` lines, too rare to report. `other`: changed patterns past `top_n` (`patterns`, `current_count`, `baseline_count`). `note`: set when the baseline sample is empty, so everything reads as new; says whether it was complete.

Rules:
- An empty `patterns` list is a valid answer, but it means only that no template changed by `change_factor` with at least `min_count` lines, not that the windows are unchanged. Report no significant log novelty, and before calling the logs unchanged check `below_min_count_patterns` (rarer changes, including new or disappeared templates) and each window's `sample_limit_reached`. Do not invent patterns, counts or causes, and do not lower `min_count` or `change_factor` to produce a finding.
- Counts are within each window's sample of at most `sample_size` lines; quote `comparison_basis`. Failed search chunks return a tool error. A truncated or capped search still returns a comparison, with `sample_limit_reached` true on that window. Under `share_of_sample`, `increased` and `decreased` mean the template's share of its sample changed, not its rate; say so.
- When either window has `sample_limit_reached`, `new` and `disappeared` mean absent from the other window's sample, not from the window; say so.
