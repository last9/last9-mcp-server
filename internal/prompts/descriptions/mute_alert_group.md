Mute EVERY alert rule in an alert group at once, or unmute them all. This applies to all current and future rules of the group until the deadline; to mute a single rule, prefer patch_alert with mute_until instead. Parameters: entity_id (required, alert group/entity UUID from get_alert_groups or get_alert_config) and until (required, Unix epoch seconds when the mute ends). Use until=0 to unmute the whole group, and a negative value such as -1 to mute indefinitely (unmute later with until=0). While the group is muted, rule-level mute_until and recurring_mute_schedule changes via patch_alert are rejected with a conflict, so unmute the group first. Confirm the scope with the user before muting a whole group. Requires editor access. Returns the group's mute record and a link to Alerting.
